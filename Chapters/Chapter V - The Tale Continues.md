## Summary

The Fellowship of the Code grew in numbers, after reliable companions have joined together to marsh onwards to Mount Doom. Flabbergasted by our hobbit craftmanship, we established ourselves as dependable and worthy of this mission. As the Fellowship left Rivendell and our adventure became more dangerous, we hobbits thought hard and heavy how we can utilize the existing functions and capabilites of our HobViewer and extend our application to raise our success rate even higher. While traveling through different areas of Middle-Earth, we realized how weather and temperatures influence not only our physical and mental state, but also how it affects our ressource management. Where there is more heat, we needed more beverages, where there are more rocky areas, we needed more food to maintain our physical reserves. Hence, we decided to extend our HobViewer with the Weather Condition Check *(WCC)* to gain insights through real-time data of each area to make planning of internal and external ressources clearer and planable.
